# Safety Disclaimer

Survey Signal is a research and survey-assist tool. It exists to help trained analysts *prioritize* ground for professional survey — nothing more.

**Survey Signal must never be used to:**

- Declare any land safe, cleared, or free of explosive hazards.
- Substitute for non-technical or technical survey conducted by an operator accredited under International Mine Action Standards (IMAS) and the relevant National Mine Action Authority.
- Guide any person to enter, cross, or work in a suspected hazardous area.

**Known limitations (fundamental, not bugs):**

- Detects **surface-laid** objects only. Buried mines — the majority in legacy minefields — are invisible to RGB imagery regardless of model quality.
- Detection rates from published benchmarks (~95% surface detection) come from seeded test fields in favorable conditions. Vegetation, weathering, and partial burial sharply reduce real-world performance.
- A missed detection (false negative) is invisible in the output. The absence of a marker means nothing.

If you have found a suspected explosive object: do not approach it. Mark the location from a safe distance and report it to local authorities or the national mine action center.

Landmines and explosive remnants of war killed or injured at least 6,279 people in 2024, most of them civilians. Treat this problem with the seriousness it demands.
